The assignment

From fragmented accounting support to a traceable year-end close.

The task was not simply to “send documents to the auditor.” The close required accounting analysis, adjustment entries, reconciliations and a structured evidence trail so each material balance could be traced from the trial balance to supporting documents and, where applicable, to settlement evidence.

Completeness gaps

Unbilled / accrued revenue, related VAT, accrued government and employee-related expenses, and other year-end items required recognition and support.

Reconciliation & classification

Bank activity, supplier settlements, payroll payments, loan balances, current/non-current classification, guarantees and legacy customer balances required review and correction.

Audit evidence volume

Hundreds of contracts, payroll records, bank statements, invoices, guarantees, tax files and samples had to be indexed and connected to the accounting records.

Professional boundary: My role was year-end close, accounting correction, reconciliation, sampling and audit support. The independent auditor is responsible for the audit opinion; this case study does not present me as the statutory auditor.
My working method

Control, traceability and fast audit response.

1. Map every audit request

Converted auditor requirements into folders and working-paper categories: revenue, banks, payroll, assets, taxes, guarantees, loans and company documents.

2. Reconcile before submitting

Compared ledger balances to statements, schedules and supporting documents; corrected accounting entries instead of merely explaining unexplained differences.

3. Build transaction-level evidence

Linked invoices to payments, project revenue to contracts and progress evidence, payroll to bank payments, and loans to lender schedules.

4. Capture year-end completeness

Recognized accrued revenue, accrued costs, statutory liabilities and employee-benefit obligations in the correct reporting period.

5. Document adjustments

Kept correction entries, explanations and source documents together to make the audit trail reviewable and repeatable.

6. Deliver a final close pack

Supported the final audited financial-statement package with reconciled schedules and a structured evidence archive.

Not just “audit assistance” — a controlled financial-close project.
