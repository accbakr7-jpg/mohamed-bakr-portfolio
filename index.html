Featured Case Study · Liquidity Rescue

6-Month Liquidity Rescue & Scenario Plan

A cash-flow crisis was diagnosed across 12 anonymized projects, with monthly deductions reaching 27.4% and producing an estimated SAR 188,996 monthly cash deficit.

Diagnosis: 97.2% of deductions were concentrated in only four projects, while pricing exposure added further pressure on liquidity.

Solution: Three quantified recovery scenarios combining deduction reduction, cost savings, debt-service relief and payment prioritization.

Modeled outcome: The realistic scenario turns monthly cash flow positive from month 3, while the stronger scenario reaches positive cash flow from month 2.
